Services

From tracking a dossier to acting on it

Our services are built around the four files on our Regulatory Watch — the EES/ETIAS border regime, EUDR, PPWR and the Industrial Accelerator Act — though the same approach extends to other files as needed.

🔔

Regulatory Monitoring & Alerts

Ongoing tracking of your chosen dossiers against their official sources (EUR-Lex, the EP Legislative Observatory, Council and Commission publications), with alerts as soon as a status, date or substantive provision changes — not weeks later.

📋

Impact & Alignment Assessment

When a file changes, we map the change against your operations, products or supply chain to determine precisely what it affects and what your business needs to do to stay aligned.

💬

Briefings & Advisory

Plain-language briefings on a specific dossier — current status, what's likely to change next, and how it compares to earlier drafts or previous deadlines.

🎯

Custom Dossier Tracking

Following a file that isn't on our public Regulatory Watch? We can track any EU legislative or implementing file relevant to your sector on the same basis.
