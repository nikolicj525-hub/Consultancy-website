About Us

Deep on a small number of files, rather than shallow on all of them

We'd rather track four EU dossiers closely than a hundred loosely. That focus is what lets us tell you the moment something changes — and what it actually means.

Our approach

[Your Consultancy Name] was founded around a simple observation: businesses don't need another generic compliance checklist, they need to know precisely when the handful of EU files that actually affect them move — a date slips, a threshold changes, a committee amends a proposal — and what that means in practice.

We currently follow four files closely: the EES/ETIAS border regime, the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR), the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), and the Industrial Accelerator Act (IAA) proposal. Each is tracked against its official source — EUR-Lex, the European Parliament's Legislative Observatory, Council and Commission publications — and reviewed regularly so our Regulatory Watch page reflects the current state, not a snapshot from months ago.

Where we're headed

Tracking is the foundation. The next layer is alignment: helping you translate each change — a new application date, a simplified due-diligence requirement, an amended threshold in a still-moving proposal like the IAA — into a concrete view of what it means for your own compliance position.
